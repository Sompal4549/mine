## 7. Jewellery Care Tips

- Store gold ornaments separately in soft cotton or velvet-lined boxes to prevent scratches.
- Put jewellery on last (after perfumes, hairsprays, cosmetics dry).
- Remove gold rings/bangles before heavy chores, gardening, swimming in chlorinated pools, or handling harsh detergents.
- Fasten chain clasps before storing to prevent tangling.
- Plain 22K Gold: Soak in warm water with mild baby shampoo 10 mins, brush with ultra-soft baby toothbrush, rinse, pat dry with lint-free cloth.
- Kundan & Polki: Never immerse in water. Clean only with dry soft muslin cloth.
- Pearls & Emeralds: Avoid hot water/steam/ultrasonic cleaners. Wipe gently with barely damp microfibre cloth.
- Diamonds & Rubies: Gently degrease with lukewarm soapy water.
- Get gemstone settings checked every 12 months (annual prong inspection).
- Restringing pearl/bead necklaces every 1 to 2 years.
- Complimentary in-store ultrasonic buffing for Shri Swami Jewellers ornaments anytime.
